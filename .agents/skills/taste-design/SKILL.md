# Taste Design: Luxury Boutique Hospitality & Editorial Aesthetics

## 1. Aesthetic Direction for The Unique Haven Homes (TUHH)
The Unique Haven Homes represents premium, handpicked stays in Lucknow (Gomti Nagar, Sushant Golf City, Shaheed Path).
The design language must evoke:
- **Heritage Meets Modern Elegance**: Royal Nawabi hospitality fused with contemporary 5-star luxury living.
- **Architectural Immersion**: Highlighting plush interiors, terrace pools, private cinemas, lush lawns, and curated comforts.
- **Effortless Trust**: Transparent pricing, verified reviews, seamless WhatsApp booking, and personal concierge service.

---

## 2. Signature Color Palette
- **Obsidian Noir (Canvas Base)**: `#0B1120` / `#0F172A` — Deep, quiet night-sky dark that makes property photography pop.
- **Warm Linen / Champagne (Card Surfaces & Light Accents)**: `#FAF7F2` / `#F3EDE2` — Tactile, organic paper feel.
- **Heritage Terracotta / Royal Amber (Primary Brand Accent)**: `#C2410C` / `#D97706` — Warm, welcoming, reminiscent of Lucknow terracotta and warm evening lamps.
- **Emerald Garden (Amenity Accents)**: `#059669` / `#10B981` — Signifying private gardens, eco-luxury, and freshness.
- **Muted Platinum (Borders & Subtle Dividers)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark; `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` on light.

---

## 3. Editorial Typography System
- **Display Headlines**:
  - `font-family: 'Cormorant Garamond', serif;` or `font-family: 'Playfair Display', serif;`
  - Style: Italicized accents, generous letter spacing, commanding serif presence.
- **Functional & UI Copy**:
  - `font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif;`
  - Crisp readability, geometric proportions, high legibility on mobile screens.
- **Editorial Badges & Tags**:
  - Subtle pill badges with uppercase tracking:
    `font-size: 0.72rem; letter-spacing: 0.14em; text-transform: uppercase; font-weight: 600;`

---

## 4. Photography & Media Presentation
1. **Aspect Ratios**:
   - Hero Property Images: `16:9` or `21:9` for panoramic grandeur.
   - Property Grid Cards: `4:3` or `3:2` golden ratio.
2. **Image Treatments**:
   - Subtle vignette gradient overlay at the base for clear text legibility (`linear-gradient(to top, rgba(11, 17, 32, 0.9) 0%, rgba(11, 17, 32, 0) 60%)`).
   - Smooth scale zoom on hover (`transform: scale(1.04); transition: transform 0.6s cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`).
3. **Amenity Badges**:
   - Minimalist iconography with concise labels (e.g., 🏊 Private Pool, 🎬 Home Theatre, ⚡ 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, 🍳 Private Chef, 🚗 Valet Parking).

---

## 5. Direct Booking & Trust Triggers
1. **No-Middleman Price Guarantee**: Highlight direct-booking savings over Airbnb/MakeMyTrip/Booking.com (Zero service fees, best rate direct).
2. **Instant WhatsApp Concierge**: Instant one-click connection to property host with pre-populated stay dates and guest count.
3. **Live Guest Testimonials**: Authentic guest stories, star ratings, and dates with clean quote typography.
