# UI UX Pro Max: Design Intelligence & Reasoning Rules

## 1. Industry-Specific Hospitality Heuristics
- **High-Conversion Frictionless Journey**: A guest should be able to understand the property, verify amenities, check photos, and reserve or connect via WhatsApp in under 2 minutes.
- **Visual Evidence Over Clutter**: Guests care about bedroom comfort, bathroom cleanliness, private lawn/pool, and parking. Never feature utility appliances (washing machines, electrical panels, buckets) on prime hero or showcase grids.
- **Trust Architecture**: Clearly display MCA Government registration, Superhost rating badges (4.94★), verified guest review counts, and zero middleman fee guarantee.

## 2. Signature Motion Effects
- **Image Expansion Text Blend**: Hero background expands smoothly as user scrolls, while hero text softly blends away with backdrop mask, seamlessly revealing the villa.
- **Sticky Scroll Room Narrative**: Large high-res architectural photograph stays anchored on the sticky stage while storytelling room blocks scroll past, crossfading photos in sync with text.
- **Subtle Typing Effect**: Character-by-character typing with natural human pacing and pause on the primary brand statement to bring the page to life without overwhelming the user.

## 3. Strict Responsiveness & Performance
- **Fluid Layouts**: Full support for all device viewports: Surface Pro (960px), iPad Air/Pro (820px-1024px), Laptops (1280px-1440px), Ultrawides, and compact phones (iPhone SE 375px).
- **Fast First Paint**: Core hero visual loads eager; all gallery/secondary photos use `loading="lazy"`.
- **Zero Raw Leaks**: Floating sticky bars and overlays must never leak raw unstyled text on any screen size.
